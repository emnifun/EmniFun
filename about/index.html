💡

FiveWink

▾

Standard (Classic) Mode Playable

In Standard mode, your goal is to deduce a hidden five-letter English word within 6 normal attempts. Color-coded letter feedback guides your subsequent guesses.

Letter Evaluation Feedback

W
Blue ("correct"): The letter is in the secret word and in the exact position.
I
Orange ("wrong-position"): The letter is in the secret word, but belongs in a different position.
N
Gray ("absent"): The letter does not appear in the secret word (or duplicate count is exceeded).

Duplicate Letters Rule

Evaluations use standard two-pass matching. Exact position matches are colored blue first. Remaining letters are matched left-to-right as orange up to the number of times the letter actually appears in the secret word. Any additional duplicate letters are marked gray.

Progressive Clue Assistance

Up to 5 progressive clues can be revealed instead of taking a normal guess. Requesting a clue consumes that attempt row on your game board. Clues provide helpful contextual hints about the target word.

Optional 7th-Guess Stage

If all 6 normal attempts are used without solving the word, you enter the 7th-guess stage. You may enter a final guess or choose to skip. Solving on the 7th guess earns a Failed — Close result; an incorrect 7th guess or skip records a Failed — Hard result.

Mode Locking

You can change game modes before making any gameplay move. As soon as you submit your first guess or reveal your first clue, Standard mode locks for that puzzle session.

Authoritative Vocabulary

Every submitted guess must exist in EmniFun's authoritative 16,273 five-letter English vocabulary dictionary. Invalid words and random letters are rejected without penalizing your attempt count.

Hard Mode: Strict Playable

Hard Mode: Strict is an elevated challenge designed for solvers who prefer rigorous logical deduction without assistive hints. Every guess must strictly obey all previously uncovered constraints.

Clues Disabled

Progressive clues are not available in Hard Mode: Strict. All attempts must be made using pure word deduction.

Three Strict Deduction Constraints

Starting from your second guess, the game engine enforces three mandatory constraints on every submission:

  1. Exact Position Requirement (Blue Letters): Any letter confirmed blue in a previous guess must be used in the exact same position in all subsequent guesses.
  2. Position Restriction (Orange Letters): A letter confirmed orange in a previous guess cannot be placed into any position where it was previously confirmed orange.
  3. Letter Retention & Multiplicity (Orange & Blue): Any letter confirmed orange or blue must be present in the new guess. If an earlier guess confirmed multiple occurrences of a letter (e.g. two E's confirmed), all subsequent guesses must contain at least that many occurrences.

Validation Feedback & Seventh Stage

If a candidate guess violates any strict constraint, an explanatory alert indicates which requirement was not met (e.g. "Position 2 must be A" or "Letter E cannot be in position 3"). The guess is rejected without consuming an attempt. If you reach the optional 7th-guess stage, your 7th guess must also satisfy all Hard Mode constraints.

Mode Locking

Selecting Hard Mode: Strict and submitting your first guess permanently locks your session into Hard Mode. Switching back to Standard mode is disabled for that daily puzzle.

Player Guide & FAQ Verified & Planned Details

Find answers to common questions about accounts, gameplay limits, device synchronization, and platform features.

Can I play FiveWink as a guest without creating an account? Verified

Yes. Anyone can play FiveWink immediately without an account. Guest sessions and statistics are stored in your local browser session. Guests enjoy unlimited puzzle replays.

How do puzzle attempts and replays work for logged-in accounts? Verified

To preserve challenge integrity, authenticated players are limited to one official play per daily puzzle. Once a puzzle is completed while logged in, replay is locked, and your final result and solution are saved.

Does my active game progress save if I refresh or switch devices? Verified

Yes, for logged-in accounts. Your guesses, letter evaluations, and locked mode are synchronized with the cloud database in real time. If you switch devices or refresh, logging in resumes your in-progress puzzle.

How does sign-in work? Do I need a password? Verified

EmniFun uses passwordless Google Sign-In (Google Identity Services). Your account features a custom Gamer Tag and an encrypted Recovery Vault with two emergency recovery keys.

What timezone is used for daily puzzle updates? Verified

Daily puzzles follow canonical India Standard Time (IST — UTC+5:30), updating each day at midnight (00:00 IST / 18:30 UTC).

What dictionary is used for valid guesses? Verified

FiveWink validates all guesses against a curated authoritative dictionary of 16,273 five-letter English words. Proper nouns and arbitrary letter combinations are rejected without penalizing an attempt.

Can I replay past puzzles from previous days? In Development

The Puzzle Archive Explorer is currently under development. In the future, authenticated accounts will be able to browse and replay past published puzzles.

Are streaks and statistics synced permanently across devices? In Development

In the current prototype, gameplay statistics are tracked within the active browser session. Persistent, cloud-synchronized player statistics across devices are planned for an upcoming release.

Will there be multiplayer, friend challenges, or leaderboards? Planned

Global leaderboards, speed challenges, and social play are on the EmniFun roadmap but are not yet implemented.
