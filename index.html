Welcome to EmniFun

A simple home for word and puzzle games. FiveWink is now playable as a functional prototype.

Wink your way to the word.

Games

Three independent game slots, with FiveWink currently playable.

Statistics

FiveWink results are tracked in the current browser session. Backend persistence comes later.

Puzzle Archive

The future archive will manage draft, scheduled, published, and archived puzzles.

This prototype uses one local published FiveWink puzzle and does not expose an editable public archive.

Login

Production login and registration will be connected to a secure backend in a future phase.

Future registration will use a username and password. Passwords will be handled by the backend, never stored in frontend source code.
